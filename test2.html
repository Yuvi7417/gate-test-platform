Not checked Not attempted

For the circuit shown, the switch is closes at t = 0 (after having been open for a very long time).
The current i (t ) is given by (for t ≥0):

Correct answer: D
Solution

Since the switch was open for a long time, the capacitor was initially discharged. After the switch  is closed, the capacitor looks initially like a short circuit thus drawing a current of 1 mA. As time  progresses the capacitor charges and thus approaches an open circuit so that i (∞) = 0.
The RC time  constant is obtained from the two 20 k Ωresistors in parallel and the 10 μF capacitor, or RC = 10–1  sec.
