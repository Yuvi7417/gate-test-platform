What is Calculus?

Calculus is the branch of mathematics that studies rates of change and accumulation using limits, derivatives, and integrals.

Why Calculus is Important?

  • Used in optimization problems
  • Important in machine learning
  • Foundation for engineering analysis

Limits

  • Concept of approaching a value
  • Used to define derivatives and continuity

Continuity

  • Function has no breaks
  • Limit exists and equals function value

Differentiation

  • Rate of change of a function
  • dy/dx notation

Applications of Derivatives

  • Maxima and minima
  • Tangent and normal
  • Curve analysis

Integration

  • Reverse of differentiation
  • Area under curve

Definite and Indefinite Integrals

  • Indefinite: ∫f(x)dx
  • Definite: ∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx

Applications of Integration

  • Area and volume
  • Physics problems

Applications of Calculus

  • Optimization
  • Engineering analysis
  • Economics and modeling

Common GATE Question Types

  • Limits and continuity
  • Differentiation problems
  • Maxima and minima
  • Definite integration

Common Mistakes in Calculus

  • Incorrect limit evaluation
  • Errors in differentiation rules
  • Wrong integration techniques
