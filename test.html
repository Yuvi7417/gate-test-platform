Solution: { x + k y = 1 k x + y = − 1 \begin{cases}x+ky=1\\kx+y=-1\end{cases} {x+ky=1kx+y=−1​, with determinant Δ = 1 − k 2 \Delta=1-k^2 Δ=1−k2.

No solution requires Δ = 0 \Delta=0 Δ=0 (i.e. k = ± 1 k=\pm1 k=±1) and the two equations to actually be inconsistent (parallel but not identical lines):
- k = 1 k=1 k=1: equations become x + y = 1 x+y=1 x+y=1 and x + y = − 1 x+y=-1 x+y=−1 — parallel, contradictory → no solution.
- k = − 1 k=-1 k=−1: equations become x − y = 1 x-y=1 x−y=1 and − x + y = − 1 -x+y=-1 −x+y=−1, which is just the first equation multiplied by − 1 -1 −1 — the same line → infinitely many solutions, not "no solution."

What is Calculus?

Calculus is the branch of mathematics that studies rates of change and accumulation using limits, derivatives, and integrals.

Why Calculus is Important?

  • Used in optimization problems
  • Important in machine learning
  • Foundation for engineering analysis

Limits

  • Concept of approaching a value
  • Used to define derivatives and continuity

Continuity

  • Function has no breaks
  • Limit exists and equals function value

Differentiation

  • Rate of change of a function
  • dy/dx notation

Applications of Derivatives

  • Maxima and minima
  • Tangent and normal
  • Curve analysis

Integration

  • Reverse of differentiation
  • Area under curve

Definite and Indefinite Integrals

  • Indefinite: ∫f(x)dx
  • Definite: ∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx

Applications of Integration

  • Area and volume
  • Physics problems
